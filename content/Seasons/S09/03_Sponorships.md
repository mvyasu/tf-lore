# Seasonal Sponsors

## OSPUZE

>**Who needs water when OSPUZE brings the surge?**
>**Fuel up and make the Arena shake.**
>
>Rooted in gaucho grit and pure adrenaline, OSPUZE's sugar-free Pro Juice is the push, the pop, the charge you need when the crowd counts on you.
>
>"Energy isn't the point. What you do with it is" - Sofia Pretronelle, CEO
>
>**POP. POUR. PERFORM**
### Backstage

>**BACKGROUND**
>OSPUZE began as a premium Argentinian beverage brand before evolving into a global icon of energy and endurance.
>
>Their signature sugar-free Pro Juice and Tiger Juice are the lifeblood of the Arena, energizing contestants, fans, and entire broadcasts.
>
>**CEO**
>Sofia Pretronelle turned OSPUZE from a local legend into a global powerhouse.
>
>In the Arena or the boardroom, she doesn't follow trends. She sets them. Ask her what OSPUZE stands for and she'll laugh: "Momentum. Everything else is packaging."

## VOLPE

>**VOLPE turns sweat into strength. And strength into victory.**
>
>From back-alley gyms to the world stage, VOLPE turns effort into excellence, and regional champions into legends using proprietary VR training technology and grit.
>
>"Victory is simply. Do the work. Do it again. Then do it better." Mackenzie Lapis, CEO
>
>**FIGHT. FALL. RISE.**
### Fine Print

>By signing with VOLPE, you agree to follow Mackenzie's morning routine: wake before dawn, train until it hurts, then arrive early enough to make everyone else feel late. Failure to comply will be logged as "rest day". And there are no rest days.
### Backstage

>**BACKGROUND**
>A once mismanaged Anglo-American sports brand risen from obscurity thanks to MultiCo's investment.
>
>VOLPE markets a new era of athletic dominance with training programs, Arena tech, and gear that blurs the line between muscle and machine. It's not about style. It's about stamina.
>
>**CEO**
>Mackenzie Lapis, 3-time Trans-Atlantic World Heavyweight Boxing Champion, won a majority stake in VOLPE through sheer will and one legendary fight.
>
>She leads from the floor, not the office, charging into every action and human interaction with relentless willpower and combativeness.
>
>She still trains every morning and still hates shoes that squeak. No hesitation, no compromise, no suits. Just results.

# Past Sponsors

## ALFA ACTA

>**Let's face it, the Arena isn't safe.**
>**Unless you're back by the best.**
>
>ALFA ACTA's unrivaled weapon and defense systems empower contestants to take control of every moment in the Arena.
>
>"Our pitch is simple really: safety at all costs."
>Junior McJohnson, CEO
>
>**MINIMIZE RISK. MAXIMIZE FIREPOWER.**

### Backstage

>**BACKGROUND**
>ALFA ACTA started small with suburban panic buttons, home turrets and undisclosed private contracts. Decades later they're fortifying entire nations, and most recently: the virtual world.
>
>For ALFA ACTA, THE FINALS is just another kind of field: one with global broadcast rights.
>
>**CEO**
>Junior McJohnson doesn't smile much, but when he does, people move out of the way.
>
>He drives ALFA ACTA with the same energy he brings to family game night: three contingency plans, one panic room, and a firm handshake.
>
>His office has a framed quote from his father: "Hope is for people who ran out of ammunition."

## ISEUL-T

>**Never let the spotlight catch you unstyled.**
>**ISEUL-T turns slip ups into legends.**
>
>Born in Seoul and reborn in the clouds of VR, ISEUL-T merges high fashion with virtual identity, dressing contestants who see the Arena as their limitless runway.
>
>"To wear ISEUL-T is to broadcast identity" Yun Beom-seok, CEO
>
>**TRANSCEND REALITIES.**
### Backstage

>**BACKGROUND**
>ISEUL-T redefined fashion for a world unbound by matter.
>
>Every design exists in both Meatspace and digital form, letting Contestants express themselves across every layer of reality.
>
>More than clothing, ISEUL-T crafts identity: wearable icons of who you are, and who you want to be.
>
>**CEO**
>Yun Beom-seok is a name wrapped in legend. Rarely seen in person, he appears only as holographic projections in ISEUL-T signature cyan.
>
>Some say he hides for security, others say to maintain the mystery. Most just want to know how old he really is.

## HOLTOW

>**Where there's value, there's risk.**
>**And where there's risk, there's HOLTOW.**
>
>Whether it's a broken bone or a broken firewall, HOLTOW is there to make sure your next disaster is nothing more than a line item.
>
>"Risk is inevitable, and recovery is optional. We sell the option." Quemby Rutile, CEO
>
>**DIGITALLY INSURED. VIRTUALLY SECURED.**
### Backstage

>**BACKGROUND**
>HOLTOW began by insuring lost passwords and corrupted avatars-small policies for small disasters.
>
>But they soon realized that in a world where nothing is safe, everything can and should be insured.
>
>**CEO**
>Quemby Rutile has never been surprised. A man of numbers and immaculate bowties, he claims to dream entirely in risk assessments.
>
>During a speech, a light fixture fell directly beside him mid-sentence Without flinching, he adjusted his tie and remarked, "Calculated incident, category C."

## TRENTILA

>**Slightly unstable. Shockingly good.**
>**TRENTILA fuels legends.**
>
>From the Trans-Atlantic Landfill to your table, and now directly to the spotlight of the Arena.
>
>TRENTILA's genetically engineered agricultural products, including its signature Tiger Fruit, push the limits of biology, maintaining peak ripeness 100x longer than fully organic matter.
>
>"Our Products taste the same on day 200 as they did on day one" - Ana Del Mar, CEO.
>
>**REAL FRUIT. BUT BETTER.**
### Backstage

> **BACKGROUND**
> TRENTILA started as an experimental farm built on recycled soil and stubborn optimism. Now it's a biotech empire with labs in orbit and patents older than some countries.
> 
> Tiger Fruit was their first masterpiece with its sweeter flesh, sharper color and uncanny shelf life.
> 
> **CEO**
> Ana Del Mar leads TRENTILA with the fire of a pioneer and the calculating mind of a scientist. A geneticist turned executive, she grey TRENTILA from a back-alley greenhouse into a global symbol of synthetic perfection.
> 
> Her office smells faintly of citrus and antiseptic, and her favorite phrase is printed on every labworker's badge: "If nature couldn't keep up, that's on her."

# VAIIYA

>**Always watching. Always protecting.**
>
>From grid to mind, VAIIYA's cyber security product suite shields the Arena and the World from instability interference and the unexpected.
>
>Because harmony and balance come from unanimous togetherness and collective peace of mind.
>
>"That strange thought? That lapse in focus? With VAIIYA, it's no longer your problem." Sal Scoria, CEO
>
>**WE. AS ONE.**
### Backstage

> **BACKGROUND**
> Founded as a private guarding firm and reborn for the digital age, VAIIYA excels in full-spectrum cybersecurity and virtual safe-zones that span the frontiers of data, reality and identity.
> 
> Governments call it unhackable. Philosophers call it dangerous. VAIIYA calls it progress.
> 
> **CEO**
> Sal Scoria, an AI-protocol at the helm of VAIIYA, embodies absolute vigilance. When he speaks, he is never alone.
> 
> Every word is cross-checked through thousands of predictive subroutines before release. His message? "We deliver harmony and peace of mind. We protect you from everyone. Including yourself."

## DISSUN

>**When the world demands power, DISSUN delivers.**
>
>DISSUN is the pulse beneath civilization. Their Redstream energy network fuels the World, the Arena, and every known corner of the virtual realm.
>
>"If it's important, we'll power it." Damien Vossler, CEO
>
>**POWER IS POWER**
### Backstage

>**BACKGROUND**
>Originally a private grid contractor, DISSUN grew by doing what governments couldn't: keeping the lights on when everything else failed.
>
>Today, they power everything from city grids of the Meatspace to the heart of THE FINALS broadcast.
>
>**CEO**
>Damien Vossler, DISSUN's commanding CEO, sees power as both art and science.
>
>He doesn't want the world to run smoothly. He wants it to run through him.
>
>With a reputation for ambition that rivals his company's reach, Vossler leads with a conviction that power is power, and the world runs thanks to those bold enough to wield it.

## ENGIMO

>**ENGIMO threads your truth through every system.**
>**Just make sure your mind doesn't fall behind the network**
>
>From its labs in Kyoto to the Arena's core, ENGIMO's quantum network upholds nanosecond latency, keeping every broadcast flawless and absolute.
>
>"In a world full of noise, we hear everything that matters." Ichikawa Natsume, CEO
>
>**BEYOND BOUNDR1fS. WITHIN REACH.**
### Backstage

>**BACKGROUND**
>ENGIMO began with a simple question: what if information was transferred instantly and didn't need translation?
>
>Decades later, their network underpins MultiCo's infrastructure, powering perceptions and reality itself—from the global grids to the very code of THE FINALS.
>
>**CEO**
>Ichikawa Natsume once said that when everything works together, there's no such thing as distance.
>
>She's evolved ENGIMO from a modest, convectional structure to an omnipresent communications machine, shaping cognition for billions of people.
>
>Natsume leads with the quiet certainty of someone who already knows the answer, and quite possibly wrote the question too.
