Towards the end of Season 6, billboards for Hydra Q began to appear around the arenas. However, the actual pictures for said media were missing which was mentioned in the [Sponsor Rivalry Vignette](11_Sponsor_Rivalry.md) a few days later after they first appeared.

![[S06_HydraQ_Billboards_01.png]]

The billboards contained the following text:

```
MultiCo_OS V.105.05

SAFEZONE
HYDRA_Q.JPEG
SAFEZONE

HYDRA_Q_LOGO.JPEG
SysError_Code_*F1|35-N07_7011|)
```

This is the first time that `MultiCo OS` has appeared instead of `MultiCoDOS`. Besides that, the text at the bottom right reads `File Not Found` in leet speak.
